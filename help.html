Tips and Tricks

Loading and saving data

Data is saved automatically while you're working within your browser's local storage. You do not need to be connected to the Internet to work on a worksheet.

That is only temporary storage while you're working. If you exit your browser or navigate away, when returning to the PetroCAL app your worksheet will be as you left it. However, if you clear your browser cache, or your browser or OS automatically cleans temporary files, this data is likely to be lost - so you should not rely purely on the in-browser temporary saving.

For more permanent storage, use the Save button to download a data file. You can save this anywhere on your device, email it to others, or upload to cloud storage e.g. Microsoft OneDrive or Google Drive.

Using the Load function allows you to open a data file and populate your worksheet.
